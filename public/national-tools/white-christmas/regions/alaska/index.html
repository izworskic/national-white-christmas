White Christmas in Alaska

Alaska contains several winter climates: relatively mild maritime Southeast, snowy Southcentral, very cold Interior valleys and Arctic communities with long-lived snow cover.

What drives Christmas snow here?

Persistent cold

Across much of Interior and northern Alaska, once snow cover establishes it can persist for months.

Maritime influence

Coastal Southcentral and Southeast locations can experience rain, mixed precipitation and thaw cycles.

Terrain & local wind

Mountains, gaps and coastal exposure create large local differences in snowfall and snow retention.

Why local matters: Alaska should not be treated as one guaranteed-snow block. Southeast maritime locations and Interior/Arctic locations have fundamentally different winter behavior.
