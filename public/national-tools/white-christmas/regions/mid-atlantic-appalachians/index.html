White Christmas in the Appalachians

The Mid-Atlantic sits near the southern edge of reliable winter snow, but the Appalachians add elevation and colder microclimates that can sharply increase the chance of snow on the ground.

What drives Christmas snow here?

Elevation

Higher ridges and mountain towns retain snow better than adjacent Piedmont and coastal locations.

Storm track

Small shifts in coastal or inland lows can decide whether a location gets snow, mixed precipitation or cold rain.

Warm-sector intrusions

Mild air ahead of storms can erase existing snowpack quickly in lower elevations.

Why local matters: For Christmas travel in this region, mountain elevation often matters more than the state name. A ridge town and a nearby valley can have very different outcomes.
