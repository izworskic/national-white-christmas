White Christmas in the high Southwest

The Southwest is a reminder that latitude alone is not enough. High-elevation communities can have meaningful Christmas snow while nearby desert basins remain mild and dry.

What drives Christmas snow here?

High elevation

Places above roughly 6,000–7,000 feet can support persistent winter snowpack despite their southern latitude.

Storm scarcity

Snow can be episodic, so the timing of a few winter systems has outsized influence.

Strong sun & dry air

Snow can melt or sublimate quickly between storms, especially on exposed slopes.
