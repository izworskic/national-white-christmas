Great Lakes & Ohio Valley

White Christmas around the Great Lakes

This is one of the most spatially variable Christmas-snow regions in the country. Lake-effect belts can build durable snow only a short drive from cities with much thinner or intermittent cover.

What drives Christmas snow here?

Lake effect

Wind direction and fetch can concentrate snow into narrow belts east and southeast of the lakes.

Water temperature & air mass

Cold air over relatively warm lake water can produce repeated snow bands early in winter.

Thaws & rain

Southern and lower-elevation parts of the region can lose an established pack quickly during a December warm spell.
