Rockies & Mountain West

White Christmas in the Rockies

In the Mountain West, elevation often matters more than latitude. High-country towns can hold deep winter snow while nearby lower valleys and Front Range cities fluctuate between snow cover and bare ground.

What drives Christmas snow here?

Elevation

Higher terrain is colder and receives more snow, producing huge local differences within the same metro or county.

Chinook & downslope warming

Strong downslope winds can rapidly melt or scour snow east of mountain ranges.

Pacific storm cycles

Repeated mountain storms can build durable snowpack well before Christmas.

Why local matters: Use a town or ZIP rather than assuming a nearby mountain's snowpack represents the valley where you will actually spend Christmas.
