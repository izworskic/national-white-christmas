White Christmas in the Pacific mountains

The Pacific states contain some of the snowiest mountains in the country and some famously mild lowland cities. Elevation and the freezing level determine which side of that divide a location falls on.

What drives Christmas snow here?

Freezing level

A small temperature change can shift precipitation from valley rain to mountain snow.

Pacific storm supply

Moist Pacific systems can build enormous mountain snowpack when temperatures are cold enough.

Rain-on-snow risk

Warm atmospheric-river events can compress or melt lower-elevation snowpack quickly.

Why local matters: Seattle, Portland and Sacramento are poor proxies for nearby mountain destinations. The regional question is fundamentally an elevation question.
