White Christmas in the Northeast

The Northeast ranges from relatively marginal coastal cities to snowy interior valleys, northern New England and higher terrain. Latitude, elevation, storm track and how long existing snow survives all matter.

What drives Christmas snow here?

Interior cold

Northern and inland areas are more likely to preserve snowpack through mild interruptions than the coastal plain.

Elevation

The Adirondacks, Greens, Whites and other higher terrain can behave very differently from nearby lower cities.

Coastal storms

A well-timed nor’easter can transform the final-week outlook, while rain or marine warmth can erase snow near the coast.

Why local matters: A broad “Northeast” average hides too much. Buffalo lake effect, Burlington cold, Boston marine influence and Adirondack elevation are different Christmas-snow problems.
