Southern edge of the snow belt

Where a rare White Christmas can happen

A White Christmas is uncommon across much of the South, but not impossible. The best chances tend to occur in higher elevations, the southern High Plains and years when Arctic air overlaps a well-timed storm.

What drives Christmas snow here?

Arctic-air timing

Cold air usually has to arrive at exactly the right time because the background climate is too mild to preserve snow for long.

Elevation & High Plains

The Texas Panhandle, eastern New Mexico and southern Appalachian high country can behave much colder than nearby lowlands.

Fast melt risk

Even when snow falls, strong sun and mild temperatures can remove it before December 25.

Why local matters: This is the region where climatology and the actual final-week pattern diverge most sharply. A low historical chance can still produce a memorable one-off Christmas.
