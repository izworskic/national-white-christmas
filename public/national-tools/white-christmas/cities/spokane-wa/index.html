What drives Christmas snow in Spokane?

Inland cold

Distance from the Pacific produces colder winter temperatures than western Washington.

Pacific storm moisture

Storms crossing the Cascades can still deliver snow when the inland air mass is cold enough.

Chinook and warm-sector risk

Periodic warm southwest flow can cause melt or mixed precipitation.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Spokane?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
