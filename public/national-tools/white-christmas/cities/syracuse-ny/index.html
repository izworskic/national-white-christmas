What drives Christmas snow in Syracuse?

Lake Ontario snow

West and northwest flow can produce repeated lake-effect snow into central New York.

Inland cold

Distance from the immediate Atlantic coast reduces marine moderation and helps existing snow survive.

Warm intrusions

Strong southerly flow ahead of eastern storms can still bring rain or a quick thaw before Christmas.

What matters most this year?

For Syracuse, watch whether December establishes a base early. A modest pack plus repeated cold is often more important for Christmas morning than whether one large storm occurs on the holiday itself.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Syracuse?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
