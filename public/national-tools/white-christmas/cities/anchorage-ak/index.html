What drives Christmas snow in Anchorage?

Southcentral cold

Long winter nights and generally cold December temperatures favor persistent snow.

Cook Inlet moderation

Maritime influence can raise temperatures and introduce mixed precipitation during strong warm storms.

Terrain effects

Chugach Mountains strongly influence snowfall and create sharp local differences around the metro.

What matters most this year?

The main question for Anchorage is usually snowpack depth and whether any unusual warm event is expected, not whether winter has begun.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Anchorage?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
