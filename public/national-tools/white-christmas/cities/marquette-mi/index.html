What drives Christmas snow in Marquette?

Lake Superior snow

Cold northwest flow over Lake Superior can add frequent early-winter snow.

Persistent cold

Upper Peninsula temperatures often allow snowpack to survive for long stretches.

Local exposure

Wind direction and immediate shoreline versus inland elevation still create meaningful differences.

What matters most this year?

The key question is usually not whether snow has fallen somewhere around Marquette, but how deep and durable the city snowpack is after any pre-Christmas warm spells.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Marquette?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
