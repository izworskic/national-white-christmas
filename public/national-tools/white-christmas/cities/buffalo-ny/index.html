What drives Christmas snow in Buffalo?

Lake-effect placement

Cold west or southwest flow across Lake Erie can focus heavy snow into narrow bands that create large differences across the metro.

Snowpack durability

Once a deeper pack is established, persistent cold can carry it toward Christmas even if no storm falls on December 25.

Thaw risk

A mild southwest flow or rain event can rapidly reduce snow depth, especially closer to the lake and in the urban core.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Buffalo?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
