Will Burlington have a White Christmas?

Burlington has a cold northern climate, but Lake Champlain and local elevation differences make Christmas snow less uniform than a statewide Vermont average suggests.

What drives Christmas snow in Burlington?

Northern latitude

Long December nights and frequent subfreezing weather help preserve existing snow.

Lake Champlain influence

The lake can locally moderate temperatures and affect where snow bands or mixed precipitation develop.

Green Mountain elevation

Nearby higher terrain often holds snow more reliably than the lower Champlain Valley.

What matters most this year?

The biggest question is often whether the Champlain Valley has a lasting base, not whether the surrounding Green Mountains are snowy. The live city estimate should therefore be checked separately from nearby ski-country conditions.

Burlington White Christmas questions

Does snow have to fall on Christmas Day in Burlington?

No. A White Christmas means at least one inch of snow is on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall earlier and still count.

When should I trust the Burlington 2026 estimate most?

The estimate becomes more actionable as December progresses, especially when observed snowpack and the Christmas-week forecast are available.
