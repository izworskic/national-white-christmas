Boston, MA

Will Boston have a White Christmas?

Boston sits near the southern and coastal edge of dependable New England snow cover. White Christmases are possible, but the city is much more vulnerable to marine warmth and December thaws than inland New England.

What drives Christmas snow in Boston?

Coastal temperatures

Ocean influence often keeps Boston warmer than inland Massachusetts during marginal winter events.

Storm track

The exact path of a coastal low can determine snow versus rain in the metro.

Urban snow loss

Roads, buildings and warmer surfaces can accelerate melt compared with colder inland towns.

What matters most this year?

Boston is a place where the live estimate can change quickly late in December. One well-timed snowstorm can create a White Christmas, while one rain event can erase the pack just as quickly.

Boston White Christmas questions

Does snow have to fall on Christmas Day in Boston?

No. A White Christmas means at least one inch of snow is on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall earlier and still count.

When should I trust the Boston 2026 estimate most?

The estimate becomes more actionable as December progresses, especially when observed snowpack and the Christmas-week forecast are available.
