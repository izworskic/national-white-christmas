What drives Christmas snow in Colorado Springs?

High elevation

Elevation supports cold nights and snow when storms arrive.

Downslope warming

West winds descending from the Rockies can quickly warm and dry the city.

Strong sun

High-elevation sunshine can melt shallow snow efficiently even when air temperatures are cool.

What matters most this year?

A pre-Christmas storm helps only if it is followed by limited downslope warming. The live estimate should emphasize actual ground cover rather than nearby mountain snowfall.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Colorado Springs?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
