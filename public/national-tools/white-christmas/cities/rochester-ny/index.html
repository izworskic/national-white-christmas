What drives Christmas snow in Rochester?

Lake Ontario influence

Cold west or northwest flow can add lake-effect snow, but band placement often favors areas east or southeast of the lake rather than the entire metro.

Inland cold

Rochester is less marine-moderated than coastal Northeast cities, so established snow can persist through ordinary December cold.

Thaw timing

Warm southwest flow and rain can sharply reduce a shallow pack, making the final pre-Christmas thaw window important.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Rochester?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
