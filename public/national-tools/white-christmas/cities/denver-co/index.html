Denver, CO

Will Denver have a White Christmas?

Denver can be snowy in December, but its Front Range setting also produces rapid warm-ups and Chinook-style downslope winds that can erase snow surprisingly fast.

What drives Christmas snow in Denver?

Front Range storms

Upslope events can deliver substantial snow when cold air is banked against the mountains.

Downslope warming

Dry west winds can rapidly raise temperatures and accelerate melt.

Elevation contrast

Nearby foothills and mountain towns can remain snow-covered while Denver returns to bare ground.

What matters most this year?

For Denver, the timing of the last storm matters less than whether cold weather follows it. A deep-looking snowpack can shrink rapidly under sun and downslope warmth.

If Denver is marginal, where nearby may be snowier?

Boulder, CO

Evergreen, CO

Denver White Christmas questions

Does snow have to fall on Christmas Day in Denver?

No. A White Christmas means at least one inch of snow is on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall earlier and still count.

When should I trust the Denver 2026 estimate most?

The estimate becomes more actionable as December progresses, especially when observed snowpack and the Christmas-week forecast are available.
