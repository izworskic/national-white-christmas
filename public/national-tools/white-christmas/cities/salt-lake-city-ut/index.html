What drives Christmas snow in Salt Lake City?

Valley elevation

The city is high by national standards but much lower than nearby ski terrain.

Great Salt Lake influence

The lake can enhance snowfall during favorable cold northwest flow.

Strong elevation gradient

Snow depth can increase dramatically over a short drive into the Wasatch.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Salt Lake City?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
