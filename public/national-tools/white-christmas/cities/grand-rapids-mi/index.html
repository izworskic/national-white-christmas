What drives Christmas snow in Grand Rapids?

Lake-effect reach

West flow can carry snow bands inland from Lake Michigan into the metro.

Southern Lower thaws

Warm southwest flow can push temperatures above freezing for extended periods.

Band variability

Western suburbs, downtown and communities farther east can see different snow depth after the same event.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.
