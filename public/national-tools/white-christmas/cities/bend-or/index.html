Bend, OR

Will Bend have a White Christmas?

Bend's high-desert elevation and position east of the Cascades make it far more snow-favorable than Oregon's major west-side cities, though dry sunny spells can reduce shallow snow quickly.

What drives Christmas snow in Bend?

High elevation

Bend sits around 3,600 feet, supporting colder temperatures and more snow than Oregon lowlands.

Cascade rain shadow

The area is drier than the west side, so cold does not always coincide with frequent snow.

Dry sun and sublimation

Low humidity and winter sun can gradually reduce snowpack even when temperatures stay cool.

What matters most this year?

For Bend, actual observed depth is essential. Nearby mountains can be buried while town remains only lightly covered after a dry stretch.

Bend White Christmas questions

Does snow have to fall on Christmas Day in Bend?

No. A White Christmas means at least one inch of snow is on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall earlier and still count.

When should I trust the Bend 2026 estimate most?

The estimate becomes more actionable as December progresses, especially when observed snowpack and the Christmas-week forecast are available.
