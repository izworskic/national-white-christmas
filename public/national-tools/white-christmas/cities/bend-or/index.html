What drives Christmas snow in Bend?

High elevation

Bend sits around 3,600 feet, supporting colder temperatures and more snow than Oregon lowlands.

Cascade rain shadow

The area is drier than the west side, so cold does not always coincide with frequent snow.

Dry sun and sublimation

Low humidity and winter sun can gradually reduce snowpack even when temperatures stay cool.

What matters most this year?

For Bend, actual observed depth is essential. Nearby mountains can be buried while town remains only lightly covered after a dry stretch.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Bend?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
