What drives Christmas snow in Minneapolis?

Persistent cold

Long stretches below freezing reduce melt and help even modest snow depth last.

Storm supply

Cold alone is not enough; a dry early winter can still leave limited cover.

Wind and compaction

Open exposure and urban surfaces can redistribute or reduce shallow snow.

What matters most this year?

For Minneapolis, the most useful December signal is whether an established snowpack is paired with continued cold. A single warm spell matters less when the pack is deep, but more when it is marginal.

Minneapolis White Christmas questions

Does snow have to fall on Christmas Day in Minneapolis?

No. A White Christmas means at least one inch of snow is on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall earlier and still count.

When should I trust the Minneapolis 2026 estimate most?

The estimate becomes more actionable as December progresses, especially when observed snowpack and the Christmas-week forecast are available.
