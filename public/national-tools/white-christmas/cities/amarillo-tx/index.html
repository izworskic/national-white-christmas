Amarillo, TX

Will Amarillo have a White Christmas?

Amarillo is one of the better Southern Plains candidates for an occasional White Christmas because its high elevation and exposure to Arctic air make it much colder than most of Texas.

What drives Christmas snow in Amarillo?

High Plains elevation

Amarillo sits around 3,600 feet, supporting colder winter temperatures.

Arctic fronts

Strong cold surges can arrive quickly and create a window for snow and snow retention.

Rapid rebound

Dry sun and strong temperature swings can melt shallow snow soon after a storm.

What matters most this year?

Amarillo is highly timing-dependent. A snowstorm several days before Christmas may vanish, while a cold storm on December 23–24 can create a rare but genuine White Christmas.

Amarillo White Christmas questions

Does snow have to fall on Christmas Day in Amarillo?

No. A White Christmas means at least one inch of snow is on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall earlier and still count.

When should I trust the Amarillo 2026 estimate most?

The estimate becomes more actionable as December progresses, especially when observed snowpack and the Christmas-week forecast are available.
