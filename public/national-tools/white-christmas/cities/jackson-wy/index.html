What drives Christmas snow in Jackson?

High elevation

Jackson sits above 6,000 feet, supporting cold temperatures and durable December snow.

Mountain snowfall

Pacific storms crossing the Tetons can repeatedly refresh the regional snowpack.

Valley inversions

Cold air pooling can keep the town colder than nearby slopes during quiet weather.

What matters most this year?

For travelers, the question is not whether the Tetons have snow but what is on the ground in Jackson itself. The city estimate should be checked separately from ski-area totals.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Jackson?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
