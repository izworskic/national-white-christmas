What drives Christmas snow in Fairbanks?

Subarctic cold

Persistent deep cold strongly favors snow retention.

Low winter moisture

The Interior is relatively dry, so snow depth may be modest despite extreme cold.

Inversions

Strong valley inversions trap cold air and reinforce snowpack durability.

What matters most this year?

For Fairbanks, measured depth is the most useful variable. The pack may not be exceptionally deep, but December warmth is much less likely to remove it than in most U.S. cities.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Fairbanks?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
