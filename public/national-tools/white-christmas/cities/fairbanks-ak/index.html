Fairbanks, AK

Will Fairbanks have a White Christmas?

Fairbanks has a deeply continental subarctic winter. Once snow cover is established, extremely cold December temperatures make meaningful melt unlikely.

What drives Christmas snow in Fairbanks?

Subarctic cold

Persistent deep cold strongly favors snow retention.

Low winter moisture

The Interior is relatively dry, so snow depth may be modest despite extreme cold.

Inversions

Strong valley inversions trap cold air and reinforce snowpack durability.

What matters most this year?

For Fairbanks, measured depth is the most useful variable. The pack may not be exceptionally deep, but December warmth is much less likely to remove it than in most U.S. cities.

Fairbanks White Christmas questions

Does snow have to fall on Christmas Day in Fairbanks?

No. A White Christmas means at least one inch of snow is on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall earlier and still count.

When should I trust the Fairbanks 2026 estimate most?

The estimate becomes more actionable as December progresses, especially when observed snowpack and the Christmas-week forecast are available.
