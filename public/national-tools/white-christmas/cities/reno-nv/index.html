Reno, NV

Will Reno have a White Christmas?

Reno is a high desert city in the rain shadow of the Sierra. It can receive winter snow, but Christmas cover is much less dependable than nearby Tahoe and Sierra communities.

What drives Christmas snow in Reno?

High desert

Cold nights support snow, but low precipitation means storms are less frequent.

Sierra rain shadow

Many Pacific storms lose moisture before reaching Reno.

Strong sun

Dry air and sun can remove shallow city snow quickly after a storm.

What matters most this year?

Reno White Christmas questions

Does snow have to fall on Christmas Day in Reno?

No. A White Christmas means at least one inch of snow is on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall earlier and still count.

When should I trust the Reno 2026 estimate most?

The estimate becomes more actionable as December progresses, especially when observed snowpack and the Christmas-week forecast are available.
