What drives Christmas snow in Flagstaff?

High elevation

Cold nights and mountain temperatures allow snow to persist far better than in Arizona's desert cities.

Episodic storms

Southwest winter storms can be infrequent, so one or two systems can dominate the December snow story.

Strong sun

Bright, dry weather can melt exposed snow quickly between storms.

What matters most this year?

Flagstaff's Christmas odds can rise quickly after a well-timed storm and fall quickly during a dry sunny stretch. Actual snow depth is more informative than assumptions about Arizona as a whole.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.
