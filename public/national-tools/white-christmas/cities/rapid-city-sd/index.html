What drives Christmas snow in Rapid City?

Black Hills elevation

Nearby higher terrain can be deeply snowy while Rapid City itself is comparatively bare.

Chinook warming

Downslope west winds can produce rapid winter warm-ups and fast snow loss.

Upslope snow

East or northeast flow can focus heavy snow against the Black Hills and rapidly change local conditions.

What matters most this year?

Rapid City is a place where a regional snow map can mislead. Check the city itself because Black Hills snow and city snow cover can diverge dramatically before Christmas.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.

When does the Christmas forecast become useful for Rapid City?

Historical odds are useful early. Existing snowpack becomes more important inside about 45 days, and the short-range weather forecast becomes a major input only when Christmas is roughly a week away.
