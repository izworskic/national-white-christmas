What drives Christmas snow in Milwaukee?

Lake Michigan moderation

Near-shore temperatures can be milder than locations farther inland during marginal winter events.

Lake enhancement

Certain wind and storm setups can enhance snowfall near the western shore even though classic lake-effect is less persistent than on the eastern shore.

Cold after snow

Once a storm is followed by sustained cold, snow cover can persist efficiently into Christmas.

What matters most this year?

Milwaukee's best signal is the combination of an established December base and the temperature trend that follows it. A shallow pack remains vulnerable to one lake-moderated warm spell.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.
