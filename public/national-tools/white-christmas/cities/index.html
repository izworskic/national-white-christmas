Great Lakes & Ohio Valley

NY

Buffalo

Buffalo sits beside a major lake-effect snow source, so Christmas snow can be abundant while nearby places see much less. Wind direction, lake temperature, existing snowpack and December thaws all matter.

MI

Marquette

Marquette combines northern latitude, Lake Superior snow and a cold Upper Peninsula climate, making it one of the stronger U.S. city settings for persistent Christmas snow.

MI

Traverse City

Traverse City gets northern Lower Michigan cold and lake-effect snow, but Grand Traverse Bay and local wind direction produce major differences between downtown, inland hills and nearby snow belts.

MI

Grand Rapids

Grand Rapids is close enough to Lake Michigan for frequent lake-effect snow, yet far enough south that December thaws can periodically remove a promising snowpack.

IL

Chicago

Chicago can have a snowy Christmas, but its position on the southwest shore of Lake Michigan does not favor classic lake-effect snow as often as cities east of the Great Lakes.

Northeast & New England

NY

Syracuse

Syracuse is one of the snowier major cities in the eastern U.S., with Lake Ontario influence and an inland climate that often supports persistent December snow.

VT

Burlington

Burlington has a cold northern climate, but Lake Champlain and local elevation differences make Christmas snow less uniform than a statewide Vermont average suggests.

ME

Portland

Portland is far enough north for meaningful Christmas snow potential, but its immediate Atlantic location leaves it more exposed to rain, mixed precipitation and coastal thaws than inland Maine.

MA

Boston

Boston sits near the southern and coastal edge of dependable New England snow cover. White Christmases are possible, but the city is much more vulnerable to marine warmth and December thaws than inland New England.

Upper Midwest & Northern Plains

MN

Minneapolis

Minneapolis benefits from a cold continental climate that can preserve snow for long periods once a December base becomes established.

MN

Duluth

Duluth combines northern latitude, Lake Superior influence and steep local elevation changes, producing strong but highly variable Christmas snow conditions across the city.

ND

Fargo

Fargo's Christmas snow story is driven by continental cold and open Plains exposure. Snowfall totals can vary, but once a pack forms, cold often favors long retention.

Rockies & Mountain West

CO

Denver

Denver can be snowy in December, but its Front Range setting also produces rapid warm-ups and Chinook-style downslope winds that can erase snow surprisingly fast.

MT

Bozeman

Bozeman sits in a high northern valley surrounded by mountains, giving it a much colder and snow-friendlier December climate than many lower-elevation western cities.

UT

Salt Lake City

Salt Lake City sits beside some of the snowiest mountains in the West, but the valley floor is much warmer and more variable than the Wasatch high country.

ID

Boise

Boise sits in a lower Snake River Plain valley surrounded by much snowier mountains. Christmas snow in the city is possible but far less reliable than nearby high country.

Pacific Northwest & Sierra

WA

Spokane

Spokane is much more continental than Seattle or Portland, with colder winters and a meaningful chance of persistent December snow.

OR

Bend

Bend's high-desert elevation and position east of the Cascades make it far more snow-favorable than Oregon's major west-side cities, though dry sunny spells can reduce shallow snow quickly.

CA

South Lake Tahoe

South Lake Tahoe is a high-elevation Sierra community where mountain storms can build deep December snow, but rain-snow lines and warm Pacific systems still matter.

NV

Reno

Reno is a high desert city in the rain shadow of the Sierra. It can receive winter snow, but Christmas cover is much less dependable than nearby Tahoe and Sierra communities.

Southwest High Country

AZ

Flagstaff

Flagstaff is one of the clearest examples of elevation overpowering latitude. At roughly 7,000 feet, it has a genuine mountain winter despite being in Arizona.

NM

Santa Fe

Santa Fe's elevation above 7,000 feet gives it real winter cold and snow potential, but the dry Southwest climate makes persistent cover less automatic than elevation alone suggests.

Mid-Atlantic & Appalachians

PA

Pittsburgh

Pittsburgh lies near the transition between reliably cold northern winters and the more volatile Mid-Atlantic, so Christmas snow is possible but strongly dependent on late-December storm and thaw timing.

NC

Asheville

Asheville is far south, but Appalachian elevation gives it a real—though still relatively uncommon—path to Christmas snow compared with surrounding lower-elevation Southern cities.

Alaska

AK

Anchorage

Anchorage has a cold, snowy Southcentral Alaska climate, but coastal influence and occasional warm storms mean Christmas snow should still be checked rather than assumed.

AK

Fairbanks

Fairbanks has a deeply continental subarctic winter. Once snow cover is established, extremely cold December temperatures make meaningful melt unlikely.

Southern Plains & Rare-Snow South

TX

Amarillo

Amarillo is one of the better Southern Plains candidates for an occasional White Christmas because its high elevation and exposure to Arctic air make it much colder than most of Texas.

OK

Oklahoma City

Oklahoma City sits near the rare-snow edge of the national White Christmas map. Snow can occur, but lasting Christmas cover usually requires unusually well-timed Arctic cold.
