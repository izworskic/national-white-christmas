What drives Christmas snow in Leavenworth?

Cascade snow

Storms crossing the Cascades can deliver frequent winter precipitation and substantial nearby mountain snow.

Snow level

Small temperature changes can shift precipitation between valley snow, wet snow and rain.

Cold east-side air

Cold air banked east of the Cascades can keep the valley snowy even when western Washington is mild.

What matters most this year?

For a Christmas trip, check Leavenworth itself rather than relying on pass or ski-area snow. The valley can remain cold and snowy under east-side cold air, but warm Pacific systems can change conditions rapidly.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.
