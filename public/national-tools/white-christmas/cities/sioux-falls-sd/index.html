What drives Christmas snow in Sioux Falls?

Cold-air access

Arctic air can lock in quickly and preserve snow for long stretches.

Southern exposure

Warm southerly flow can produce meaningful December thaws that are less common in North Dakota.

Open terrain

Wind can compact and redistribute shallow snow, changing actual ground cover after a storm.

What counts as a White Christmas?

The standard used here is at least 1 inch of snow on the ground on December 25. Snow can fall before Christmas and still count if enough remains on the ground.
